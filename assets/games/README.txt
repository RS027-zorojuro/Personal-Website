GAME COVER ART — drop your own image files in this folder
============================================================

Same idea as assets/anime/ — these exact filenames are what the
site looks for. Anything missing falls back to a generated tile
instead of a broken image.

  01-witcher-3.jpg           The Witcher 3
  02-gta-v.jpg                GTA V
  03-skyrim.jpg               Skyrim
  04-ghost-of-tsushima.jpg    Ghost of Tsushima
  05-spider-man.jpg           Insomniac Spider-Man Series
  06-dying-light.jpg          Dying Light

Tips:
- These render fairly small (a compact sliver in each row, larger
  in the detail drawer) — a portrait or square cover crop works
  better here than a wide screenshot.
- .jpg, .png, or .webp all work; keep the filename matching exactly.
- Only use images you personally have the right to use.
